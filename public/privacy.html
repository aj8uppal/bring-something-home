Privacy & support

This game is operated by AJ Uppal. For support or privacy requests, email aj8uppal@gmail.com. Never include your recovery code in a message.

Your traveler

The game saves your traveler name, character, inventory, vault, progression, discoveries, expedition results and death history on its server. Session and recovery credentials are stored as hashes there. Your browser keeps your session, recovery code and preferences so you can return.

Shared expeditions

Your traveler name and character are visible to other players. Names can appear in the public Hall of Echoes and shared expedition recaps. Realm chat is transient. Player reports are stored privately for moderation until resolved or the associated account is deleted.

Keeping or deleting your progress

Save your private recovery code from Settings to return on another device. Anyone with that code can use your account. Use Settings → Delete this account and its progress to remove your saved progress, credentials, leaderboard records and associated reports. Deleted data can remain in rolling server-volume snapshots for up to seven days.

Hosting

The game runs on Fly.io; its portfolio entry is hosted on GitHub Pages. The game uses connection addresses for abuse prevention. Hosting providers process network and service logs under their own policies. The game has no advertising, tracking pixels, analytics, payments or third-party sign-in.
